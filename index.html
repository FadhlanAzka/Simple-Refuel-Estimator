How it works

Calculation formula

  1. Open or set your completed route in Google Maps.
  2. Read the total trip distance and enter it manually.
  3. Enter your vehicle efficiency, fuel price, and reserve margin.
  4. Calculate the recommended refuel amount and estimated cost.

Minimum fueldistance / fuel efficiency

Recommended fuelminimum fuel + reserve margin

Estimated costrecommended fuel x price per liter

This result is an estimate. Actual fuel use may vary with traffic, road conditions, driving style, and vehicle load.

Distance is entered manually. Trip history is stored in the local folder you authorize.
